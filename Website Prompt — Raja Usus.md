# Website Prompt — Raja Usus

Create a modern, premium, appetizing website for an Indonesian food brand called **Raja Usus**.

## About the Brand

**Raja Usus** is a local Indonesian food brand currently selling **premium crispy intestine (usus krispy)** products primarily offline in **Sidoarjo and Surabaya**, as well as through friends, family, and personal networks.

The brand is currently in its early stage, so the website should focus on **brand building, product presentation, trust, and generating direct orders**, rather than looking like a large established e-commerce company.

The website should make Raja Usus feel like a **small but premium local food brand with room to grow**.

---

# Product

## Premium Usus Krispy

Raja Usus produces crispy intestine using quality ingredients and a preparation process designed to reduce excess oil.

One of the product's characteristics is that the finished product is **spun to reduce excess oil**, resulting in a crispy product that feels less oily.

The product is **halal**.

Important positioning:

**Premium Usus Krispy**

The product should be presented as an indulgent, crunchy, savory Indonesian snack/food product.

Do not describe the product as a health food.

Avoid exaggerated health claims.

# UX / DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

The website must be:

- Mobile-first
- Fully responsive
- Fast
- Clean
- Premium
- Easy to navigate
- Optimized for visitors coming from Instagram

Most users are expected to visit from a smartphone.

Therefore:

- Make product photos large
- Make prices immediately visible
- Keep text concise
- Make WhatsApp CTA prominent
- Use large touch-friendly buttons
- Use sticky mobile CTA if appropriate

---

# COPYWRITING STYLE

Use Indonesian.

Tone:

- Friendly
- Confident
- Appetizing
- Modern
- Authentic

Avoid overly formal corporate language.

Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.

Do not use phrases such as:
- "Nomor 1 Indonesia"
- "Terbaik di Indonesia"
- "Paling sehat"
- "100% sehat"
- "Tanpa minyak"
- "Tanpa pengawet"

unless these claims are later confirmed.

Instead emphasize:

**Premium**

**Krispy**

**Gurih**

**Bahan berkualitas**

**Spinner untuk mengurangi minyak berlebih**

**Halal**

---

# IMPORTANT

This is an early-stage local food brand.

Do not make the website look like a giant corporation.

The desired impression is:

**"Wow, ini brand makanan lokal yang kecil tapi produknya terlihat serius dan premium."**

The website should make someone who discovers Raja Usus through Instagram think:

> "Kelihatannya enak. Harganya juga masuk akal. Saya mau coba."

Prioritize **food photography, product presentation, pricing, trust, and WhatsApp conversion** over unnecessary features.

Do not add:
- Shopping cart
- Complex checkout
- User accounts
- Product reviews
- Loyalty programs
- Blog
- Complicated e-commerce functionality

unless specifically requested later.
